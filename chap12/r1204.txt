Is our array-based implementation of merge-sort given in Section 12.2.2
stable? Explain why or why not.


Answer:

Stable sorting algorithms perserve the order of elements with the same
ordering key.


Merge-sort as implement in section 12.2.2 is stable because:
1. When merging sorted sequences, the left side is prioritized
when S1[i] == S2[j], thus perserving original ordering of elements.
(Elements in S1 appear before elements in S2 in the original 
sequence S)
