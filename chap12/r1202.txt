In the merge-sort tree shown in Figures 12.2 through 12.4, some edges are
drawn as arrows. What is the meaning of a downward arrow? How about
an upward arrow?


Answer:

Downward arrows signal a divide operation, i.e. solve
the merge-sort problem for size n/2. Upward arrows signal
the merge operation, i.e. the merge-sort operation
for two subproblems of size n/2 that produces a sorted
sequence of size n.
