What does a splay tree look like if its entries are accessed in increasing
order by their keys?

ANSWER:

Since every key that get searched becomes root in
a splay tree, that means that if keys are accessed
in an increasing order, the largest key will be root,
the second largest will be left of root, the third
largest will be left of the second largest,
while the least key will be the leftmost position.

All other positions will be in between and that
will effectively form a straight line or a doubly -linked
list.
