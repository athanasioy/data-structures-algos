Let T be a red-black tree and let p be the position of the parent of the
original node that is deleted by the standard search tree deletion algorithm.
Prove that if p has zero children, the removed node was a red leaf.


Answer:

Let w denote the deleted node. If p has zero
children after deleting node w, then that means
that p had only one children before the deletion.
That also means that w itself doesn't have any children,
since p is left childless and in a standard BST deletion,
childern of w attach to p.

Since T was a valid red-black tree before the deletion,
all nodes with zero and one children had the same
black height. If w was black, w had a black height
greater than node by 1. Also node that w itself
cannot have any children, since having any children
would either violate the black height property or
the red property (double-red).
Therefore w must have been red.
